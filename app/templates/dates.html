{{ nsr.r }} corrected photo(s) have their mtime rounded to the whole second: harmless, nothing to do

Their EXIF date (with sub-seconds) is exact, and Immich orders photos by it: the timeline is right. Cause: exiftool -P, run by fix_order.py after it set the mtime, restores the mtime with 1-second precision (with --step below 1 s, only the photos on a whole second keep an exact mtime). Running fix_order.py again would restore the fractions, but every changed mtime makes Immich re-process that photo (thumbnails, faces, search): not worth it for albums already in Immich. To avoid it for the next albums, set the mtime after the EXIF write in fix_order.py.

How Immich reacts (external library)

  • An asset is identified by its path: renaming or moving a file or folder creates a new asset (faces, albums and favorites stay with the old one).
  • A rescan compares only the mtime: when it changed, Immich keeps the asset but re-reads its dates and re-runs thumbnails, face detection (existing faces are matched again), smart search, OCR and duplicate detection.
  • A content change with the same mtime (e.g. exiftool -P) is not noticed by a rescan.
